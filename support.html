Common questions

I paid but my Pearls didn’t show up.

Stripe sends you back to the app after paying, and the Pearls are added then. If you closed the page first or ended up in a different browser, message us with your purchase code and we’ll add them for you.

My Pearls disappeared.

Pearls, game saves and settings are saved in the browser on your phone. They’re removed if you clear your browser data, use private browsing or switch phones or browsers. Message us with your purchase code and we’ll restore Pearls you bought.

Who processes my payment?

Pearl packs are processed securely by Stripe. Your receipt email shows the name that will appear on your statement. We never see or store your card number.

I saw a page with a restaurant’s name on it. Is that their app?

No. Pages that carry a local business’s name are demonstrations we made to show the owners. They aren’t the business’s official pages, the business hasn’t endorsed them, and no rewards are earned or redeemed on them. If it’s your business and you’d like the page changed or removed, message us and we’ll do it promptly.

A stamp card promised me a reward. Who gives it to me?

The business or person who made the card. Stamp cards, link pages and shop websites are made by other people with our free tools. We don’t write or review them and can’t make anyone honor a reward.

How do I report a page made with your tools?

Message us with its link and what’s wrong with it. We can block a page from loading on our site if it breaks our terms.

Refund policy

  • Pearls and other in-game items are digital and are added to your browser as soon as you pay.
  • If a purchase never arrived, you were charged twice, or you bought something by mistake and haven’t used it, contact us within 30 days and we’ll either add what you paid for or refund you in full.
  • Items you’ve already used, such as Pearls spent on boosts, aren’t refundable, except where the law requires.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually take 5–10 business days to appear.
  • Pearls and in-game items have no cash value and can’t be exchanged for food, drinks, merchandise or money.
