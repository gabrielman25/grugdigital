Players

The Arcade, your avatar and Pearls

I paid but my Pearls didn't show up

Stripe sends you back to the site after paying, and the Pearls are added then. If you closed the page first or ended up in a different browser, message us with your purchase code and we'll add them for you.

My Pearls, characters or game saves disappeared

Pearls, saved outfits, game saves and settings are kept in the browser on your device, not on our servers. They're removed if you clear your browser data, use private browsing, or switch devices or browsers.

Message us with your purchase code and we'll restore Pearls you bought. We can't bring back characters or game saves, so download any character you want to keep.

Who processes my payment?

Pearl packs are processed securely by Stripe, and the Grug Gold membership by Gumroad. Your receipt email shows the name that will appear on your statement. We never see or store your card number.

What does it cost to download my avatar?

Making and saving characters is free, and so is playing as them in the Arcade. Downloading one as a rigged Roblox R6 file costs 25 Pearls, once per character: downloading the same character again later is free. The shirt, pants and face pictures are always free.

Everyone gets free Pearls for visiting each day, and new players start with some. The price is shown before you confirm.

How do I cancel Grug Gold, or use it on another device?

Grug Gold is a monthly membership on Gumroad. Cancel any time from the "Manage membership" link in your Gumroad receipt email. To switch on your perks on a device, paste the license key from that email into the Pearls page in the browser you play in. A key works on up to two devices.

A game won't load, or runs slowly

The games are 3D and run in your browser, so they need a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Close other tabs, and if a game has a Game Settings button, try a lower 3D resolution. If it still won't load, tell us the game, your device and your browser.

Game developers

The agent studio and asset packs

My AI agent couldn't finish the character

The Avatar Studio for agents runs in a browser, so the agent needs one it can control, such as Claude in Chrome. The outfit is painted in Google Gemini, so you need to be signed in to Gemini in that same browser. An agent that can only fetch pages can send you a link that opens the studio with your character set up, and you finish the last steps by hand.

The agent guide has every step. Claude, Gemini and other AI tools are run by their own companies, so we can't see or fix what happens inside them.

The export says I don't have enough Pearls

Exporting a character costs 25 Pearls, kept in your own browser. Agents are told to ask you before spending them, and never to buy them for you. You can get more on the Pearls page.

I bought an asset pack and need help

Our asset packs are sold on itch.io, and that's where you download your files. Each pack's page says what's included and how you may use it. For a problem with a pack, or a refund, message us with the pack's name and the email you used on itch.io.

Where did the Building Studio go?

We retired it in November 2026 to put the work into the Avatar Studio. Buildings you already downloaded are yours to keep using.

Restaurants

Stamp cards, websites and the dashboard

I saw a page with a restaurant's name on it. Is that their app?

No. Pages that carry a local business's name are demonstrations we made to show the owners. They aren't the business's official pages, the business hasn't endorsed them, and no rewards are earned or redeemed on them. If it's your business and you'd like the page changed or removed, message us and we'll do it promptly.

A stamp card promised me a reward. Who gives it to me?

The business or person who made the card. Stamp cards, link pages and shop websites are made by other people with our free tools. We don't write or review them and can't make anyone honor a reward.

I lost my dashboard, card or website

The restaurant tools have no accounts and no server: everything you enter is saved in the browser on the device you used, and your card and website ride in their own links. If that browser's data was cleared, we can't bring it back. Save a backup file from the dashboard, and keep your card's and website's links somewhere safe.

How do I report a page made with your tools?

Message us with its link and what's wrong with it. We can block a page from loading on our site if it breaks our terms.

Refund policy

For Pearls and other things bought on our site. Asset packs are sold on itch.io: message us for a refund on a pack.

  • Pearls and other in-game items are digital and are added to your browser as soon as you pay.
  • If a purchase never arrived, you were charged twice, or you bought something by mistake and haven't used it, contact us within 30 days and we'll either add what you paid for or refund you in full.
  • Items you've already used, such as Pearls spent on boosts, aren't refundable, except where the law requires.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually take 5–10 business days to appear.
  • Pearls and in-game items have no cash value and can't be exchanged for food, drinks, merchandise or money.
