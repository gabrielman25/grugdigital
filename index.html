How points work

Points and the Pass

Every meal earns points. Every point unlocks play.

One account works at every partner restaurant, so your points follow you around town.

2

Earn points

Every visit adds points to one balance that works at every partner.

3

Spend them on play

Unlock levels, items and new games here, or redeem them for food at the restaurant.
