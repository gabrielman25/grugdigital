Download

In Roblox Studio, import the .glb with the 3D Importer. Roblox shrinks textures over 1024 pixels. One grid square is one metre (about 3.5 studs).

How it works

  • Build: set the building's width, depth and height, then pick a roof (flat, gable, hip or shed), its height and direction. Roofs sit flush with the walls so nothing hides them. Everything stays chunky, because thin geometry smudges in the painted texture.
  • Doors and windows: pick how many floors, where the door goes and how many windows each wall gets. They're drawn as blue and cyan rectangles for Gemini to paint over.
  • Paint: describe the building in one box. The studio draws a colour-coded reference sheet (back view left, front view right, both at 45° and tilted down) and writes the prompt. Paste both into Gemini.
  • Line up: paste Gemini's picture back. The studio fits it to the silhouette, one view at a time.
  • Finish: every surface is projected back onto the model and baked into a retro texture with bevelled edges. Download the .glb.

Camera

  • Drag to orbit, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, F to fit.
