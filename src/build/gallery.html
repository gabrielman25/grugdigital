Example buildings

Each one started as a chunky grey box with a roof and colour-coded doors and windows. Gemini painted it from a single reference sheet, and the studio baked the painting onto the model as a retro texture with bevelled edges. Open any of them as a template and make it your own.

Use as template

How it was made

1. Reference sheet. Back view left, front view right. Grey walls, red roof, yellow trim, blue doors, cyan windows.

2. Painted in Gemini. The sheet plus the studio's prompt, pasted into Gemini (Nano Banana). No editing.

3. Baked. Lined up by silhouette, projected onto the model and finished with the Grug PS1 look.
