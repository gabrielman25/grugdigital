Your itch.io page as a shop

Type your itch.io username. Customers walk round your shop: your asset packs boxed on plinths, your games on arcade cabinets, your tools in vending machines, with live prices and sales. Out the front door is a mall of other creators’ stores.

Put it on your itch.io pages

Download a small zip and upload it to any of your itch.io pages. The store plays right on the page, and it updates itself: new uploads, prices and sales show up without uploading again.

  1. On itch.io, open the page and choose Edit.
  2. Set Kind of project to HTML. Your downloadable files stay on the page.
  3. Upload the zip and tick This file will be played in the browser.
  4. Under Embed options: viewport 960 × 640, tick Mobile friendly, Fullscreen button and Automatically start on page load.
  5. Save. Do the same on your other pages with their own zips.
