Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Grug Digital’s games and tools use, where it’s kept and who sees it.

Effective November 13, 2026

The short version
  • No account and no sign-up. Your Pearls, avatar, game saves and settings are saved in the browser on your device, not on our servers.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • We don’t sell your information, show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who we are

Grug Digital (“we”, “us”) is based in Katy, Texas. We make web games and free tools for local businesses and online creators, all on the Grug site. This policy covers the whole site, including the demonstration pages described below.

Information saved on your device

The site saves the following in your browser’s local storage on your phone or computer:

  • Your avatar, display name, closet and settings
  • Your birthday, which the games ask for the first time you open them (see below)
  • Game saves, scores, boosts and items
  • Pearls you’ve bought or collected, a short list of your purchases (pack, price and Stripe’s checkout reference) and a random purchase code
  • Stamp cards you’ve collected stamps on, and rewards cards, link pages and websites you’ve made
  • If you use the restaurant dashboard: whatever you enter there, which can include your staff’s names, jobs, pay rates and phone numbers, schedules, time-clock punches, temperature logs, checklists, recipe costs and permit dates

This information stays on your device and isn’t sent to us. You can delete it any time from Profile and settings, or by clearing your browser’s data for our site. Once deleted it can’t be recovered, including Pearls you bought.

Your birthday is used only on your device, to decide which games are shown there (a few games may be marked 18+). It is never sent to Grug Digital or anyone else. You can see and change it in Settings. It is deleted when you clear your browser’s data for our site; Profile → Start over keeps it, so the same games stay hidden. It is included if you export your profile to a file.

You can export your profile or a dashboard backup to a file. That file is yours: it’s saved wherever you choose and never comes to us.

If you run the restaurant dashboard, you decide what goes into it, and you’re responsible for the information you enter about your staff, including keeping the device it’s on secure. We can’t see it, back it up or recover it.

Pages that live in a link

A rewards card, link page or website made with our tools isn’t stored on our servers. Everything on it (such as a shop’s name, colours, reward text, hours, address or social media handles) is packed into the link itself. Anyone you give the link or QR code to can read what’s in it, so don’t put anything in one that you wouldn’t want shared.

Payments

When you buy Pearls, you pay on Stripe’s secure checkout page. Stripe collects your email address and payment details and processes them under Stripe’s privacy policy. Stripe shares with us your email address, what you bought, the amount, the payment status and the purchase code from your browser, so we can confirm orders, give refunds and answer questions. We keep these payment records as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal reasons.

Information we receive in other ways

  • When you contact us. If you text us or message us on Discord, we see your phone number or Discord username and your messages, and use them only to help you.
  • Activity notes. Some pages can post a short note to our private Discord server when something happens, such as “A player logged on to Chest Quest” or “A player bought 100 Pearls for $1.99.” The note never includes your name, display name, contact details or anything else about you. It’s sent from your browser to Discord, which receives your IP address the way any website does.

Services that load our pages

Our pages are hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your device’s IP address and basic browser information when your browser loads a page, and handles it under its own privacy policy. Some demonstration pages and downloaded shop websites load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the same kind of information. We don’t use analytics or advertising trackers, and none of our pages connect your browser to other players.

Demonstration pages for businesses

Pages that carry a local business’s name (its sample rewards app, game or website) are demonstrations we made to show that business’s owners. They aren’t the business’s official pages and the business hasn’t approved or endorsed them. Nothing you do on them is sent to us or to the business, no rewards are earned or redeemed there, and they don’t use Pearls.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don’t share it for advertising.
  • We don’t ask for your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Children

Our games are for a general audience and aren’t directed at children under 13. A few games may be marked 18+: they are only shown when the birthday saved on the device is 18 or over. That is a simple age screen on your device, not an identity check, and we never see the birthday. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Purchases require a parent or other adult cardholder. If you think a child made a purchase or shared information with us, contact us and we’ll help, including with a refund.

Your choices

You can play without buying anything. You can delete what’s saved on your device at any time, as described above. To ask about, correct or delete payment records or messages we hold about you, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here with a new effective date.
