Privacy policy

Privacy policy

What information Grug Digital’s site, games and tools use, where it’s kept and who sees it.

Effective November 23, 2026. Replaces the version of November 17, 2026.

The short version
  • No accounts and no sign-up. Your characters, outfits, Pearls, game saves and settings are saved in the browser on your device, not on our servers.
  • The Avatar Studio doesn’t upload your characters or pictures anywhere. If you choose to use Claude or Gemini with it, those companies handle what you give them under their own policies.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe (Pearls), Gumroad (the Grug Gold membership) and itch.io (asset packs). We never see or store your card number.
  • We don’t sell or share your information, and we don’t use advertising or analytics trackers. The ads on our pages are our own and our sponsors’ plain picture links.

Who we are

Grug Digital (“we”, “us”) is based in Katy, Texas. We make low-poly game asset packs, the Grug Avatar Studio, browser games and free tools for creators and local businesses, all on the Grug site at grugdigital.com, including the older pages we still run at the same address (together, “the site”). This policy covers the whole site, including the demonstration pages described below.

Information saved on your device

The site saves the following in your browser’s storage (local storage and IndexedDB) on your phone or computer:

  • Characters and outfits you make or save in the Avatar Studio, their pictures and clothing images, which one you play as, and your studio settings
  • Your Pearls balance, a short list of what you bought or spent them on (pack, price and Stripe’s checkout reference), which characters you’ve unlocked for download, which daily, welcome and promotional Pearls you’ve already received, and a random purchase code
  • Game saves, scores, levels, items and settings for each game
  • Whether you prefer the site light or dark
  • On our older pages: your avatar, display name, closet and settings; your birthday (see below); boosts and items
  • If you join Grug Gold: your membership’s license key, whether Gumroad last said it was active, when it started and ends, and which months’ Pearls you’ve claimed
  • Stamp cards you’ve collected stamps on, and rewards cards, link pages and websites you’ve made
  • If you use the accessory catalog or the eyewear and hair editors: favourites, designs you’ve saved and your own uploaded logos, plus preview pictures kept so they load faster next time
  • If you use the restaurant dashboard: whatever you enter there, which can include your staff’s names, jobs, pay rates and phone numbers, schedules, time-clock punches, temperature logs, checklists, recipe costs and permit dates
  • If you use the bubble tea or café dashboard: whatever you enter there, which can include your staff’s names, optional clock-in PINs (kept only as a scrambled code), schedules, time-clock punches, cash counts and tip splits, temperature, cleaning and waste logs, checklists, stock counts, suppliers’ contact details and shift notes, plus the name picked under “Who’s using this?” for the day. Each dashboard keeps its own records apart from the others. A bubble tea or café dashboard also adds your shop’s rewards card and website to the restaurant account on your device, the same as the restaurant dashboard does

This information stays on your device and isn’t sent to us. You can delete it any time by clearing your browser’s data for our site (or, on our older pages, from Profile and settings). Once deleted it can’t be recovered, including Pearls you bought and characters you made, so download anything you want to keep.

You can export your profile, a dashboard backup or a character to a file. That file is yours: it’s saved wherever you choose and never comes to us.

The Avatar Studio, Claude and Gemini

The Avatar Studio runs entirely in your browser. The characters you build, the outfits you paint and the files you download (shirt, pants and face pictures, and the Roblox R6 .glb or zip) are made on your device and aren’t uploaded to us.

Painting with Gemini. To paint an outfit, the studio copies a template picture to your clipboard and you (or Claude, if you ask it to) paste it into Google Gemini with a description. Whatever you paste or type into Gemini, and the pictures it makes, are handled by Google under the Google privacy policy and Gemini’s own terms. You bring the picture back into the studio yourself; we never see it.

Making characters with Claude. We offer a prompt you can copy into Claude, a guide Claude can read on our site, and a Claude skill you can download. If you use them, Claude (made by Anthropic) acts in your own browser on your instructions, and your chat, prompts and anything you add to Claude are handled by Anthropic under the Anthropic privacy policy. Nothing about you is sent to us when Claude uses the studio. Claude is told to ask you before spending any Pearls.

Google and Anthropic aren’t part of Grug Digital, and we don’t control or receive what you share with them.

The Grug Arcade

The arcade games run in your browser. Each one keeps its own save on your device, dresses you in the outfit you chose in the Avatar Studio, and fills the other players and characters with your other saved outfits and the game’s own. Those other players are computer-controlled: the games don’t connect you to other people, and there’s no chat. Pearls you earn in a game go into the same wallet on your device.

Your birthday (older games)

Some of our older games ask for your birthday the first time you open them. It is used only on your device, to decide which games are shown there (a few games may be marked 18+). It is never sent to Grug Digital or anyone else. You can see and change it in Settings. It is deleted when you clear your browser’s data for our site; Profile → Start over keeps it, so the same games stay hidden. It is included if you export your profile to a file.

Home-screen apps

If you add the Grug, Daypart, Bubble Tea or Café app to your home screen, the app also keeps a copy of our own pages, games, pictures and fonts on your device so it works without a connection, and checks our site now and then for new and updated files. Those are our files, not information about you, and nothing about you is sent when it checks. On iPhone and iPad an app added to the home screen keeps its own save, apart from Safari. Its Save a copy button makes a file of what the site has saved in that browser (everything listed above except Pearls) so you can load it into the app; like an export, that file is yours and never comes to us. On iPhone and iPad, removing the app from the home screen deletes what it saved.

If you run the restaurant, bubble tea or café dashboard, you decide what goes into it, and you’re responsible for the information you enter about your staff, including keeping the device it’s on secure. We can’t see it, back it up or recover it.

Pages that live in a link

A rewards card, link page, website or character recipe made with our tools isn’t stored on our servers. Everything on it (such as a shop’s name, colours, reward text, hours, address or social media handles) is packed into the link itself. Anyone you give the link or QR code to can read what’s in it, so don’t put anything in one that you wouldn’t want shared.

Payments

When you buy Pearls, you pay on Stripe’s secure checkout page. Stripe collects your email address and payment details and processes them under Stripe’s privacy policy. Stripe shares with us your email address, what you bought, the amount, the payment status and the purchase code from your browser, so we can confirm orders, give refunds and answer questions. We keep these payment records as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal reasons.

Grug Gold, our monthly membership, is sold on Gumroad. Gumroad collects your email address and payment details, charges you each month and processes them under Gumroad’s privacy policy; it shares your email address, what you bought and your membership’s status with us. Gumroad gives you a license key. When you paste it on the Pearls page, your browser sends the key to Gumroad’s license check (api.gumroad.com) to see whether your membership is active, and checks again now and then while you use the site. Gumroad receives your IP address the way any website does. The key is saved on your device and isn’t sent to us.

Asset packs on itch.io

Our asset packs are sold on itch.io (polysw.itch.io). The list on our home page is read from itch.io’s public store pages by our server; that request doesn’t include anything about you. The pack pictures load from itch.io’s image servers, which receive your IP address and basic browser information the way any website does. When you buy a pack, you do it on itch.io, under the itch.io privacy policy. itch.io may share details of your purchase with us as the seller, as that policy describes, and we use them only to provide the pack, give refunds and answer questions.

Information we receive in other ways

When you contact us. If you text us or message us on Discord, we see your phone number or Discord username and your messages, and use them only to help you.

Activity notes. Some of our older pages can post a short note to our private Discord server when something happens, such as “A player logged on to Chest Quest” or “A player bought 100 Pearls for $1.99.” The note never includes your name, display name, contact details or anything else about you. It’s sent from your browser to Discord, which receives your IP address the way any website does.

Services that load our pages

Our pages are hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your device’s IP address and basic browser information when your browser loads a page, and handles it under its own privacy policy. The arcade games load their 3D engine from cdnjs (run by Cloudflare), and some demonstration pages and downloaded shop websites load fonts from Google Fonts; those receive the same kind of information. We don’t use analytics or advertising trackers, and none of our pages connect your browser to other players.

Our socials, YouTube TVs and sponsor ads

Some pages and games show our Discord, Instagram and YouTube. To show live numbers, our server reads public information from Discord (how many members our server has, how many are online and, if the server’s public widget is on, the names and pictures of members who are online right now) and from YouTube (our newest videos). Your browser loads those pictures and video thumbnails from Discord and YouTube, or through our server, which passes them on without keeping anything about you.

The TVs in Grug City and the video window play our YouTube videos with YouTube’s player in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When a video loads, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and browser information and handles it under the Google privacy policy. If you click through to Discord, Instagram, YouTube or Roblox, those sites’ own policies apply.

Sponsor ads (such as KimiLand, our official Roblox UGC sponsor) are plain pictures and links on our pages. They don’t track you, and nothing about you is sent to the sponsor unless you click through to them.

Demonstration pages for businesses

Pages that carry a local business’s name (its sample rewards app, game or website) are demonstrations we made to show that business’s owners. They aren’t the business’s official pages and the business hasn’t approved or endorsed them. Nothing you do on them is sent to us or to the business, no rewards are earned or redeemed there, and they don’t use Pearls.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising.
  • We don’t build profiles of you or track you across other websites.
  • We don’t ask for your location, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • We don’t use third-party ad networks. The only ads are our own and our sponsors’ plain picture links.
  • We don’t use what you make in the Avatar Studio to train AI models (it never reaches us).

Children

Our games and tools are made for a general audience, and we know many people who play blocky games are young. We built the site so that nobody needs to give us personal information to use it: there are no accounts, no chat between players, and what you make stays on your device. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

  • Children under 13 should ask a parent or guardian before contacting us, buying anything, or using an outside service such as Claude, Gemini or itch.io. Those services have their own minimum ages, which may be higher than 13.
  • Purchases require a parent or other adult cardholder.
  • A few older games may be marked 18+: they are only shown when the birthday saved on the device is 18 or over. That is a simple age screen on your device, not an identity check, and we never see the birthday.

If you think a child under 13 has sent us personal information or made a purchase, contact us. We’ll delete the information and help, including with a refund.

Your rights and choices

You can use the site without buying anything or telling us who you are. You can delete what’s saved on your device at any time, as described above.

Depending on where you live (for example in California or another US state with a privacy law, or in the UK or European Union), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to or limit how we use it. The only personal information we usually hold is payment records and messages you’ve sent us. To make a request, contact us using the details below; we’ll answer within the time the law allows and won’t treat you differently for asking. Because we don’t sell or share personal information, there’s nothing to opt out of, and we treat a Global Privacy Control signal as that same choice. If you’re in the UK or EU, our legal bases are providing what you asked for (orders and support) and our legal obligations (accounting and tax records), and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Security and where data is kept

What’s saved on your device is protected by your device and browser, so keep them secure and up to date. The payment records and messages we hold are kept with the services named above and are only seen by the people at Grug Digital who need them. We and those services are in the United States, so if you use the site from elsewhere, that information is handled in the US.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects how we use information we already hold, we’ll make it clear on the site before it applies.
