Zone Royale

100-player battle royale · one island · one winner
  • Warm up on the spawn island, then jump from the plane and glide anywhere on a huge brick island.
  • Loot chests for guns from grey to gold, ammo, bandages, medkits and shield potions.
  • No building: break through walls with your pickaxe.
  • The storm closes in every half minute. Be the last one standing for a Victory Royale.
  • Solo, or Squads with three teammates who follow you.
  • You play as your own Grug character, and your other saved outfits drop in with you.
Play
Free, in your browser. Mouse and keyboard recommended.

Local Multiplayer

Play together on one screen · works with Xbox Wireless Controllers
  1. Connect an Xbox controller to your computer (USB cable or Bluetooth) and open a game.
  2. Press any button on it and pick Player 1 (it plays you) or Player 2 (a friend joins next to you).
  3. Every controller after that joins as the next player, up to 8. Extra players get your moves, aim with their own crosshair and come back to you if they wander off.
Try it in Zone Royale, Zombie Zones, Grug City RP, Grug Kingdom, Brickbound and Slide 999 KM.
Controller not showing up? Steam can hold on to controllers while it's open: quit Steam, or turn off Steam Input for that controller in Steam's controller settings, then press a button again. Other controllers often work too, but Xbox ones are the ones we test with.
