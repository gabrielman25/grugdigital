COMING SOON
Coming soon to the Grug Arcade

Turn any TV into a Grug console.

Your phone is the controller · nothing else to buy

Set your phone down under the TV, step back and play with your whole body. Jump, duck, dodge and dance: your phone's camera sees you move, and the Grug you made moves with you on the big screen.

It's a console you can take anywhere. Bring it to a sleepover, a birthday party or family game night, and the whole room can play.

Hear first on Discord Play the Arcade now

How it works

  1. 1Open it on the big screen

    Go to grugdigital.com on a smart TV, or on a laptop or computer hooked up to the TV, and pick a motion game.

  2. 2Prop up your phone

    Stand your phone under the TV with the camera facing the room, then scan the code on the screen. That links the two together.

  3. 3Step back and move

    Jump, duck, wave and dodge, and your Grug copies you. Grab your friends and take turns.

A console on the go

No console to set up, no controllers to charge, no cables to find. If there's a screen and you've got your phone, you've got a game night.

  • SleepoversSet it up in two minutes, play till lights out.
  • Birthday partiesEveryone gets a turn, and everyone can watch.
  • Family game nightGrown-ups, kids and grandparents, all on one screen.
  • Holidays awayGrandma's TV works too. Just bring your phone.
  • Rainy daysGet the wiggles out without leaving the house.
  • Clubs and hangoutsAny room with a screen becomes the game room.

For everyone. You don't have to be good at games: if you can jump, you can play. Kids, teens and grown-ups can all join in, and you play as the Grug you made in the Avatar Studio.

Questions

What do I need?

A phone with a camera and a web browser, and a big screen that can open grugdigital.com: a smart TV, or a laptop or computer hooked up to one.

Do I have to buy anything?

No controller, camera or console to buy. That's the whole idea: you already have the gear.

How much room do I need?

Enough to jump and swing your arms without bumping into anything. Move the coffee table, keep drinks away from where you play, and make sure pets and little brothers and sisters aren't right next to you.

Does the camera record me?

Motion Controls are still being built. Before they launch, this page will explain exactly what the camera sees and where it goes, in plain words. Younger players: ask a grown-up before you turn on the camera.

Which games will work with it?

Select Arcade games, starting with ones built for moving. They'll wear a MOTION badge in the Arcade, and they'll be listed on this page.

When can I play?

Soon! Join the Grug Discord to hear the moment it's ready.
