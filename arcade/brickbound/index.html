Help
Touch: left thumb anywhere on the lower left moves (up/down walks into and out of the level) · the buttons on the right attack, jump, shoot, dodge and guard · flick the stick twice to dash · hold Guard and tap an attack for magic
Move: ← → left and right · ↑ ↓ into and out of the level · tap an arrow twice quickly to dash that way, and keep holding it to run
Jump: C or Space · press again in the air to double jump · hold after the double jump to sword-spin glide · tap for a short hop
Light attack: X or left click (chain 4) · Heavy: Z: a launcher · Slingshot: D or V (juggles!)
Bubble: hold Z and X together (or right-click) · Magic ring: while the bubble is up, four spells sit around you: ↑ Magic Leap, → Bolt, ↓ Potion, ← Splash. Press that arrow, or click one
Combos: light ×4 · light, heavy (launch) · light ×2, heavy: pop-up · light ×3, heavy: elemental slam · in the air: light ×3, heavy for a meteor spike · after a hit lands, jump to cancel into the air and keep juggling
Other: F talk / open / shop · 2 or R drink a potion · T or 3 switch spell · Tab character · M menu · mouse wheel zoom
The world: keep going right. Arenas lock until every foe is down; once cleared they stay clear. Walk back any time for the shops, and for things you couldn't reach the first time.
