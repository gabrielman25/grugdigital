Slide all the way down. 999 KM. There's a checkpoint every 111 KM, so if you fall off (or touch anything red) you start again from the last one.
Yellow arrows boost you. Cyan pads bounce you. Blue strips push you sideways.

Between slides there are obbies: walk and jump across. A green $ ledge in each one needs a Speed or Gravity Coil.

Reach the bottom and walk through the golden door for FREE ADMIN. Then open the gold Admin button.
