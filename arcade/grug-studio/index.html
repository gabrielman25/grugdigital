a Grug Digital game
Touch: your left thumb moves · Jump and Roll buttons · drag anywhere to turn the camera, pinch to zoom
Move: WASD or the arrow keys · Space jump · Shift dodge roll
Camera: right-drag (or left-drag) or Q / E to turn · wheel or I / O to zoom (all the way in for first person)
Create an Avatar: the green pad, or 1 (the first slot at the bottom). The camera zooms in on you and the Avatar Studio opens. Press Wear it to come back wearing your new look
Make a Building: the blue pad, or 2. A building grows on the plot: change it with the sliders while you walk round it. You're the size of a Roblox character (1.8 m, 5.2 studs), so you see how big it really is
Chat: press /, type, Enter
Esc or the gear: the Game Menu · H: this help
