# Grug

The Grug game site: https://grugdigital.pages.dev/

Games built with Katy restaurants, one local profile (no accounts), a 3D avatar that shows up in every game, and a closet of clothes earned by playing or by eating at a partner. Hosted on Cloudflare Pages (deploys automatically on every push to `main`); everything is static HTML. The old GitHub Pages address forwards here.

## Map

- `index.html` — home (two doors side by side for restaurant owners and online creators, your avatar, then one endless carousel of every game as a bare 3D cartridge, starting on the one you played last). `games/` is the library: a looping vertical carousel of cartridge boxes with the selected game's details beside it; `games/<id>/` is each game's page. Every game is shown as a 3D **FOSSIL32** cartridge box (see below).
- `create/` — the **Create** tab: a marketplace-style asset library of free preset 3D models and scenes (search, categories, genres, themes, tags, sorting, favourites; GLB, OBJ, STL or the project file), each with a live 3D preview, and a button that opens **Model Kit** (`create/modelkit/`), our pixel-to-3D model editor. See `tools/modelkit/README.md`.
- The dock: **Home**, **Games**, **Me** (`me/`: Avatar, Shop, Profile) and **Make** (`make/`: Link page, Rewards card, 3D models). Me and Make open a tray when tapped and have a simple list page of their own; swipe sideways to move between tabs.
- **Shop mode:** arriving from a shop's rewards card link dresses the whole site in that shop's name, colour and font until a refresh (see `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Partner mode (CraftHouse mode):** a full partner's own address, `crafthouse/` for R&R CraftHouse Grill, pins their real logo in the top bar, keeps their app's tab bar under the dock, dresses every page (Games too) in their light look and typeface, and trims the site to Games and Me (Home and Make go to Games). It lasts until the tab closes or Settings → Leave. Partners are listed in `PARTNERS` in `assets/theme.js`.
- `avatar/` — the 3D avatar editor: no scrolling, swipe between sections (`avatar/nav.js`). `shop/` — every closet item and how to earn it. `profile/` — name, stats, points at each partner, settings, **export / import** of the profile as JSON (moves everything, points included, to another phone).
- `rewards/` — the **Rewards** tab: the guest's cards from every shop they've tapped in at (stamp cards saved on the phone, plus partner apps they've used), their Pearls, and **My creations** (cards they made). `rewards/create/` is the free do-it-yourself card builder for shop owners: one question per slide (name, kind of place, colour, icon, stamps, a reward from a short list, welcome stamp; anything custom points them to us), ending with a print-ready PDF (drawn in the browser, `GrugRewards.cardPdf`). `rewards/card/` is where that QR or an NFC sticker lands: a stamp once a day and 10 Pearls (at most 30 a day from stamp cards). The shop's whole setup rides in the link; nothing is stored on a server. Shared code: `assets/rewards.js` (link format, storage `grug.rewards.v1`, Pearls) and `assets/rewards.css`; QR drawing: `assets/qrcode.js` (MIT).
- `links/` — the **Links** tab, only for online creators: a free link page for their socials (X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Discord, a website), either a page of buttons or a small 3D world where each social is a room to walk into (`assets/linkworld.js`). `links/create/` is its one-question-per-slide builder, `links/page/` is what followers open (10 Pearls a day per page, sharing the 30-a-day cap with stamp cards). Only handles ride in the link (`assets/links.js`, saved pages in `grug.links.v1`).
- The rewards card builder shows each question in 3D (`assets/wizard3d.js`: the shop's storefront, then a phone with the stamp card and the reward) and ends on a café table with the printed card (`assets/cafe3d.js`). All 3D on these pages uses `assets/three.min.js` (r159, MIT) at half resolution so phones stay smooth.
- **Who's visiting:** the first visit asks Player / Restaurant owner / Game developer / Online creator (`Grug.ui.askRole`, kept in `profile.prefs.role`, changed in Settings → I'm a…). Each role sees only its dock tabs (`ROLES` in `assets/grug.js`), and pages can show or hide parts with `data-only-for` / `data-hide-for` (the home page does). Pages a guest reaches from a shop's QR pass `noRole` so nobody is quizzed at the counter.
- `partners/` — the "for restaurants" pitch, our work and contact (the old front page).
- `play/<name>/` — the partner rewards apps, moved here from their own repos: `yoshi`, `cielito`, `teahouse`, `soluna`. Each old URL redirects here and keeps `?tap=` NFC links and `#store/tab` hashes.
- `play/crafthouse/` — a demo rewards app made to pitch R&R CraftHouse Grill (one self-contained file: menu with photos, an order quiz, a visit-based Season Pass, a coin store that adds to the check, and three 3D swipe games: football, soccer, basketball). Its Grug links open CraftHouse mode.
- `play/runway/` — Runway Ready, a Grug original with no restaurant: a slim host page stands in for a rewards app (Pearls only, no partner points). Games like this have `standalone: true` in `assets/games.js`; copy the folder to host another one.
- `work/` — concept sites, screenshots and pitch packs. `community/` — neighborhood photos and the map.

## Shared code (`assets/`)

- `catalog.js` — the closet: every item, its slot, how it's earned (free / partner points / milestone). Add items here.
- `avatar3d.js` — builds and animates the blocky avatar with three.js (`three.min.js`, r159, MIT). Used by the site pages, the closet thumbnails and the match-3 games' "chef".
- `grug.css` + `theme.js` + `fogbg.js` — the look, matched to Model Kit: light greys by default (dark in Settings), Fredoka, flat accents and one accent colour per section (Home lime, Games green, Create teal, Avatar purple, Shop yellow, Profile pink), set by `theme.js` from the URL. Pages sit on a very faint static pixel pattern in the section's colour (`assets/patterns/`); the Games section alone keeps the blue smoky fog with small tumbling 3D cubes (`fogbg.js`). All 3D is drawn at half resolution. Load `theme.js` in `<head>` right after `grug.css` so the look is set before first paint.
- `grug.js` — the local profile (`localStorage` key `grug.profile.v1`), milestones, points reader, export/import, shared header + tab bar. `games.js` is the game list.
- `grug-app.js` / `grug-app.css` — the Grug layer inside a partner app (closet section, avatar chip, milestone hooks). `chef.js` — the avatar inside the match-3 games.
- `gamekit.js` — the site-wide layer inside every game (`play/<name>/game.html` loads it first): the UI size setting, important text centred and animated in, folded lobby text, the stamina bar and sprint input, hand-holding tutorials with Skip (basic lessons such as "walk with WASD" are tracked site-wide and only ever show once), the emote wheel (B), the Free Shop Token timer, crate-drop rolls for site-wide items, and 3D helpers (LOCKED / COMING SOON cubes, low-key props, flashy bot pets). Games start straight into play; their power-up shops sit behind an in-game Shop button.
- Site-wide items live in the profile (`profile.items`, defined in `grug.js` → `ITEMS`): the **Free Shop Token** drops from any crate, egg, loot box or daily gift in any game (6%, one held at a time) and makes every shop free for 3 minutes (`grug.boost.v1` holds the timer). Players use it from Settings or Profile → Inventory. Emotes (`grug.js` → `EMOTES`, animations in `sandy-runtime.js`) are picked in the avatar editor's Emotes tab (`profile.emotes`).
- `icons.js` — the site's one flat icon style: the @icons simple set, plus Phosphor Icons 2.1.1 (MIT) bold, including the shop icons (`sh-…`) rewards cards use.
- `fossil32/` — the FOSSIL32 boxes: `fossil32.js` (built from `tools/fossil32/src/`, three.js r170 bundled in) turns every `[data-f32="<id>"]` tile into a floating 3D cartridge box. Tapping one darkens and blurs the page, pulls the cartridge out and shows the game panel; Play puts the cartridge in the console, zooms into the screen and opens the game. `Fossil32.rack(el, state)` draws the Games page carousel and `Fossil32.launch(id)` plays straight from it. `Grug.ui.boxTile(game)` writes the tile. Rebuild with `cd tools/fossil32 && npm install && npm run build`.
- `boxart/` — each game's 1580×950 cover art (the avatar in an action pose over a shot of the game) and back-of-box screenshots. Made with `tools/boxart/` (see its README).

## Adding a game or partner app

1. Drop the app at `play/<name>/index.html` and add it to `assets/games.js` (title, partner, `storageKey`, thumbnail). Give it its FOSSIL32 box: a retro title and tagline, `features`, `box` (catalog code, cartridge colour, title font) and box art with the avatar in an action pose. The full checklist is in `CLAUDE.md` → "Adding a game", and the art tools in `tools/boxart/README.md`.
2. Load the Grug scripts in the app and call `GrugApp.init({...})` at the end of its script; call `GrugApp.onCheckin()` on check-ins and `GrugApp.onScore({score, level})` when a run ends. Add a `<div id="closet" class="grug-closet" hidden></div>` in the Rewards tab. The match-3 and Teahouse apps show the pattern.
3. Add its closet items to `assets/catalog.js` with `get: {type:'points', app:'<name>', cost}` or `{type:'milestone', app:'<name>', stat, value, text}`.
