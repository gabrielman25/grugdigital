# Grug

The Grug game site: https://grugdigital.pages.dev/

Games built with Katy restaurants (each one a standalone game, each venue with its own rewards app that can wrap the whole site), one local profile (no accounts), a 3D avatar that shows up in every game, and a closet of clothes earned by playing or by eating at a partner. Hosted on Cloudflare Pages (deploys automatically on every push to `main`); everything is static HTML. The old GitHub Pages address forwards here.

## Map

- `index.html` — home (two doors side by side for restaurant owners and online creators, your avatar, then one endless carousel of every game as a bare 3D cartridge, starting on the one you played last). `games/` is the store, laid out like a big PC game store (featured carousel, capsule rows, categories from each game's `tags`, a Discovery Queue, a tabbed list with previews, and a search page with tag and filter sidebars kept in the URL); hearting a game adds it to `games/favorites/`, a looping vertical carousel of those games' cartridge boxes with the selected game's details beside it. `games/<id>/` is each game's page. Every game is shown as a 3D **FOSSIL32** cartridge box (see below).
- `create/` — the **Create** tab: a marketplace-style asset library of free preset 3D models and scenes (search, categories, genres, themes, tags, sorting, favourites; GLB, OBJ, STL or the project file), each with a live 3D preview, and a button that opens **Model Kit** (`create/modelkit/`), our pixel-to-3D model editor. See `tools/modelkit/README.md`.
- The dock: **Home**, **Games** (Games: the store; Carts), **Me** (`me/`: Avatar, Shop, Profile) and **Make** (`make/`: Link page, Rewards card, 3D models). Games, Me and Make open a tray when tapped; Me and Make have a simple list page of their own; swipe sideways to move between tabs.
- `games/carts/` — **Carts**: quick one-tap (or one-hold) 3D games in a vertical swipe feed like Reels, built with the Grug Micro kit (`grug-game-kit/micro`). Listed for the store's search in `assets/carts.js`, covers in `assets/carts/`. See `CLAUDE.md` → Carts.
- **Shop mode:** arriving from a shop's rewards card link dresses the whole site in that shop's name, colour and font until a refresh (see `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Partner mode (a brand wrap):** a venue's own address (`crafthouse/`, `coco/`, `teahouse/`, `soluna/`, `rodeo/`, `alchemy/`) puts the venue's logo, coin balance and app tab bar on every page, dresses the whole site (Games too) in its look and typeface, and trims it to playing and dressing up. Its game list holds every standalone game with the venue's own first, and the venue's coins pay in its own games. It lasts until the tab closes. Partners are listed in `PARTNERS` in `assets/theme.js`.
- **Rewards apps:** the button at the top of the Games store lists every venue's rewards app (menu, Season Pass, coins); hidden inside a brand wrap.
- `avatar/` — the 3D avatar editor: no scrolling, swipe between sections (`avatar/nav.js`). `shop/` — every closet item and how to earn it. `profile/` — name, stats, points at each partner, settings, **export / import** of the profile as JSON (moves everything, points included, to another phone).
- `rewards/` — the **Rewards** tab: the guest's cards from every shop they've tapped in at (stamp cards saved on the phone, plus partner apps they've used), their Pearls, and **My creations** (cards they made). `rewards/create/` is the free do-it-yourself card builder for shop owners, laid out as a conversation with a premium AI assistant (there's no AI: every line is written in the page, `assets/cardchat.css`). The Grug stegosaurus "thinks", then streams each question; answers are picked from a tray of tiles at the bottom (or typed, matched to a tile), and the shop's name goes in a prompt bar. Tapping an earlier answer changes just that one. Questions: name, kind of place, colour, lettering, icon, stamps, a reward from a short list, welcome stamp (anything custom points them to us), ending with a print-ready PDF (drawn in the browser, `GrugRewards.cardPdf`). `rewards/card/` is where that QR or an NFC sticker lands: a stamp once a day and 10 Pearls (at most 30 a day from stamp cards). The shop's whole setup rides in the link; nothing is stored on a server. Shared code: `assets/rewards.js` (link format, storage `grug.rewards.v1`, Pearls) and `assets/rewards.css`; QR drawing: `assets/qrcode.js` (MIT).
- **Restaurant account:** making a rewards card also makes a restaurant account on the device (`assets/business.js`, `grug.business.v1`). Switch between it and the player account from the menu under the avatar. In it, the site opens on the **Dashboard** (`business/`, the Daypart app: schedule, recipe costs, checklists, temperatures, permits, time clock, staff), and the dock is Dashboard, **Rewards card** and **Website** (`business/site/`, edit the shop's one-page site made from its card). The site's public link is `site/#s=…` (the whole site rides in the link), or download it as one `index.html` for any host.
- `restaurant/` — the link for restaurant owners: name the restaurant, pick what it serves, and one tap makes the rewards card, website and dashboard, then a short tour of the dashboard (with example data). Add `?name=Rosa%27s%20Tacos&type=f&owner=Rosa` to set it up for a particular owner (types: c café, t boba & tea, b bar, f restaurant, k bakery, o other).
- `links/` — the **Links** tab, only for online creators: a free link page for their socials (X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Discord, a website), either a page of buttons or a small 3D world where each social is a room to walk into (`assets/linkworld.js`). `links/create/` is its one-question-per-slide builder, `links/page/` is what followers open (10 Pearls a day per page, sharing the 30-a-day cap with stamp cards). Only handles ride in the link (`assets/links.js`, saved pages in `grug.links.v1`).
- The rewards card builder's answers carry a live 3D picture (`assets/wizard3d.js`: the shop's storefront, then a phone with the stamp card and the reward) and ends on a café table with the printed card (`assets/cafe3d.js`). All 3D on these pages uses `assets/three.min.js` (r159, MIT) at half resolution so phones stay smooth.
- **Who's visiting:** the first visit asks Player / Restaurant owner / Game developer / Online creator (`Grug.ui.askRole`, kept in `profile.prefs.role`, changed in Settings → I'm a…). Each role sees only its dock tabs (`ROLES` in `assets/grug.js`), and pages can show or hide parts with `data-only-for` / `data-hide-for` (the home page does). Pages a guest reaches from a shop's QR pass `noRole` so nobody is quizzed at the counter.
- `partners/` — the "for restaurants" pitch, our work and contact (the old front page).
- **Games are standalone.** Every game lives at `play/<dir>/`: its `game.html` under a thin host page over `assets/gamehost.js` (Back, the game's name, Pearls and, in its venue's wrap, the venue's coins). Grug originals (`original: true`): `runway`, `chestquest`, `glowupspa`, `petmountainrescue`, `mysteryblasters`. Venue games: `stealasip` and `takeatea` (Teahouse Express), `sunrise` (Soluna Coffee), `rodeowash` (Rodeo Express), `sugaralchemist` (Alchemy Bake Lab).
- **Rewards apps** (`play/<venue>/`, built from `tools/partner-app/`): `coco`, `teahouse`, `soluna`, `rodeo`, `alchemy` (menu, a "what should I order?" quiz, a visit-based Season Pass, coins and app themes, and the site's games on a cartridge shelf). Each has a short address (`<venue>/`) that opens its brand wrap. NFC links (`?tap=`) stamp a visit, and the old apps' game links forward to the standalone games.
- `play/crafthouse/` — R&R CraftHouse Grill's demo app from the older template, with its three 3D swipe games still inside (`inApp: true` in `games.js`). `play/yoshi/` and `play/cielito/` are the older match-3 rewards apps (unlisted games), reachable from the Rewards apps list.
- `tools/partner-app/` — the template every partner rewards app is built from: the shared app (no game room of its own; its games are the site's standalone games), one folder per partner (`config.json`, `quiz.json` + menu data) and `build.py <id>` → `play/<id>/index.html`. Its README is the step-by-step for a new venue, with the browser checklist.
- `work/` — concept sites, screenshots and pitch packs. `community/` — neighborhood photos and the map.

## Shared code (`assets/`)

- `catalog.js` — the closet: every item, its slot, how it's earned (free / partner points / milestone). Add items here.
- `avatar3d.js` — builds and animates the blocky avatar with three.js (`three.min.js`, r159, MIT). Used by the site pages, the closet thumbnails and the match-3 games' "chef".
- `grug.css` + `theme.js` + `fogbg.js` — the look, matched to Model Kit: light greys by default (dark in Settings), Fredoka, flat accents and one accent colour per section (Home lime, Games green, Create teal, Avatar purple, Shop yellow, Profile pink), set by `theme.js` from the URL. Pages sit on a very faint static pixel pattern in the section's colour (`assets/patterns/`); the Games section alone keeps the blue smoky fog with small tumbling 3D cubes in dark mode (`fogbg.js`; a plain page in light mode). All 3D is drawn at half resolution. Load `theme.js` in `<head>` right after `grug.css` so the look is set before first paint.
- `grug.js` — the local profile (`localStorage` key `grug.profile.v1`), milestones, points reader, export/import, shared header + tab bar. `games.js` is the game list.
- `grug-app.js` / `grug-app.css` — the Grug layer inside a partner app (closet section, avatar chip, milestone hooks). `chef.js` — the avatar inside the match-3 games.
- `gamekit.js` — the site-wide layer inside every game (`play/<name>/game.html` loads it first): the UI size setting, important text centred and animated in, folded lobby text, the stamina bar and sprint input, hand-holding tutorials with Skip (basic lessons such as "walk with WASD" are tracked site-wide and only ever show once), the emote wheel (B), the Free Shop Token timer, crate-drop rolls for site-wide items, and 3D helpers (LOCKED / COMING SOON cubes, low-key props, flashy bot pets). Games start straight into play; their power-up shops sit behind an in-game Shop button.
- Site-wide items live in the profile (`profile.items`, defined in `grug.js` → `ITEMS`): the **Free Shop Token** drops from any crate, egg, loot box or daily gift in any game (6%, one held at a time) and makes every shop free for 3 minutes (`grug.boost.v1` holds the timer). Players use it from Settings or Profile → Inventory. Emotes (`grug.js` → `EMOTES`, animations in `sandy-runtime.js`) are picked in the avatar editor's Emotes tab (`profile.emotes`).
- `icons.js` — the site's one flat icon style: the @icons simple set, plus Phosphor Icons 2.1.1 (MIT) bold, including the shop icons (`sh-…`) rewards cards use.
- `fossil32/` — the FOSSIL32 boxes: `fossil32.js` (built from `tools/fossil32/src/`, three.js r170 bundled in) turns every `[data-f32="<id>"]` tile into a floating 3D cartridge box. Tapping one darkens and blurs the page, pulls the cartridge out and shows the game panel; Play puts the cartridge in the console, zooms into the screen and opens the game. `Fossil32.rack(el, state)` draws the Games page carousel and `Fossil32.launch(id)` plays straight from it. `Grug.ui.boxTile(game)` writes the tile. Rebuild with `cd tools/fossil32 && npm install && npm run build`.
- `boxart/` — each game's 1580×950 cover art (the avatar in an action pose over a shot of the game) and back-of-box screenshots. Made with `tools/boxart/` (see its README).

## Adding a game or partner app

1. A game: put it at `play/<dir>/game.html` (built with the game kit) and copy another game's `index.html` next to it, changing the `GrugGameHost({...})` call (id, title, Store, save key, and `brand` if a venue made it). Add it to `assets/games.js` (`play`, `game`, `save`, `venue` or `original: true`) with its FOSSIL32 box: a retro title and tagline, `features`, `box` and box art with the avatar in an action pose. The full checklist is in `CLAUDE.md` → "Adding a game", and the art tools in `tools/boxart/README.md`.
2. A venue's rewards app: follow `tools/partner-app/README.md` (config, menu, quiz, build, the `PARTNERS` entry and the short address). It shows up in the Games page's Rewards apps list on its own.
3. Closet items go in `assets/catalog.js`: `{type:'milestone', app:'<game app id>', stat, value, text}` (`plays`, `best`…).
