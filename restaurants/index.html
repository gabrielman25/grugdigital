How it works

About a minute from your restaurant's name to a card you can print.

  1. Name your restaurant

    Pick what you serve and tap once. Your card, website and dashboard are made together.

  2. Print the QR

    Put the card by the register, or on your tables. Guests scan it with their phone's camera.

  3. Watch regulars come back

    Each visit is a stamp. A full card is the reward you chose.

There are no accounts and no server behind this. Your shop's setup rides in its link and is saved on your device, so keep a backup from the dashboard's settings.
