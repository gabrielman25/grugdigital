How it works

  1. Name your restaurantPick what you serve and tap once. Your card, website and dashboard are made in about a minute.
  2. Print the QRPut the card by the register, or on your tables. Guests scan it with their phone's camera.
  3. Watch regulars come backEach visit is a stamp. A full card is the reward you chose.

There are no accounts and no server behind this: your shop's setup rides in its link and is saved on your device, so keep a backup from the dashboard's settings.
